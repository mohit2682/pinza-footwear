About Pinza Footwear · Established 2005 · ISO 9001:2015

A trading house
run by shoe people

Established in 2005 in Delhi, Pinza Footwear is a trusted supplier, wholesaler and trader of safety shoes, law enforcement boots and uniform shoes, alongside ladies chappals and sandals. We sell in bulk — retailers, distributors, industrial buyers and government departments all buy from the same desk.

We buy from a vendor base built over two decades. Vendors are chosen on market credibility, financial stability, cost effectiveness, transparent dealing and after-sales support, and our own inspectors visit their premises to check machinery and finished goods against our quality norms. A dedicated warehouse lets us hold bulk consignments and turn large orders around without the usual delays.

Over the last two decades the business has grown from a single product into a multi-brand distribution organisation. Today we are one of the leading distributors of Allen Cooper — the best-known safety shoe brand in India — as well as a channel partner for several others, and we hold ISO 9001:2015 certification for our quality management system.

The work has stayed the same throughout: get the right footwear, certified and inspected, to the people who need it — whether that is a shop floor in a steel plant or a shelf in a retail store.

Our proprietor, Mrs. Neerja Mehani, has spent over a decade in the footwear trade and built the company from a single-brand operation into a portfolio house. Our CEO, Mr. Prem Mehani, brings twenty-five years of footwear manufacturing behind him — which is why we talk about lasts, sole densities and toe-cap tolerances rather than just SKUs.

Let there be no bare feet — we know how these shoes are made, so we know when a consignment isn't right.

We represent our partner brands to dealers, private industry and government buyers. That includes bidding in central and state tenders on their behalf, preparing technical compliance documentation, and helping buyers work out which specification actually suits their hazard — furnace heat, chemical splash, electrical work, or wet and oily floors.

Over 150 client accounts sit on our books today, including IOCL, DHL, Trelleborg, Tricolite, Checkmate and the Indian Air Force. A dedicated marketing team works across our footwear categories, a sales desk keeps in regular contact with existing accounts, and a logistics and administration team handles the movement of goods.

We also work upstream. With our CEO's manufacturing background, we advise our principals' product development teams and have helped develop high-performance shoes in their factories that meet international standards — in several cases replacing expensive imported footwear outright.

Quality control is our own, not outsourced. Our inspectors visit vendor premises during production and re-inspect goods on arrival at our store. More recently we've moved into e-commerce with a direct-to-consumer store, so a single pair is as welcome as a thousand.
